About

The Cloud Continuum

The Cloud Continuum (CC) is emerging as a new, elastic, and adaptive paradigm that seamlessly integrates a wide spectrum of computational and ownership models. It encompasses a spectrum of deployment models spanning things–edge–cloud, operating across heterogeneous service abstractions, such as Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), Function as a Service (FaaS), cloud-native Container as a Service (CaaS), and platform engineering frameworks. By exploiting these highly distributed resources, the CC enables previously unachievable elasticity, adaptability, and dynamism across a multitude of use cases and evolving workloads.

This paradigm shift opens the doors to unprecedented opportunities in domains such as smart cities, autonomous systems, Industry 5.0, immersive applications, next-generation connected services, data-intensive AI workflows, and 6G/NGN architectures. Applications can increasingly move, expand, shrink, specialize, or adapt depending on latency, cost, resilience, energy efficiency, and contextual constraints.

Motivation

Research Challenges & Opportunities

This powerful model also deeply transforms how we design, develop, test, deploy, and operate modern services. The inherent heterogeneity of resources, platforms, and cloud models forces researchers and practitioners to rethink traditional DevOps pipelines, orchestration frameworks, and operational processes. Complexity may hinder development speed, amplify infrastructure fragility, and exacerbate issues related to distributed reliability, performance, data consistency, operational observability, and continuous delivery.

Moreover, the intrinsically distributed nature of the Cloud Continuum introduces critical challenges such as partial outages, intermittent connectivity, multi-tenant interference, dynamic relocation of workloads, and the difficulty of guaranteeing SLOs/SLA compliance across highly variable environments. These issues create new research opportunities at the intersection of DevOps, cloud systems, distributed computing, AI/ML, IoT-native design, and next-generation networking.

Workshop Aims

Goals

This workshop aims to bring together researchers, practitioners, and innovators to present the latest advances in architectures, methodologies, platforms, and tools that support DevOps practices across Cloud Continuum environments. We welcome both conceptual and applied contributions, including empirical studies, new frameworks, innovative prototypes, and visionary use cases.
Topics of Interest

Topics of Interest

The topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the following categories.

🏗️Foundations & Infrastructure for the Cloud Continuum

  • Architectures enabling seamless edge–cloud integration
  • Multi-cloud/multi-edge orchestration layers
  • Energy-aware and sustainability-driven infrastructures
  • Operations in massive IoT deployments
  • Protocols and mechanisms for resilient continuum operations
  • Distributed data management platforms for continuum applications
  • TinyML model deployment and runtime management on microcontrollers and ultra-constrained devices
  • GPU/TPU/NPUs and hardware-accelerated execution along the continuum
  • Distributed service meshes and programmable data planes
  • Serverless/FaaS enablement across heterogeneous resources

⚙️DevOps Methodologies, Pipelines & Platform Engineering

  • DevOps pipelines tailored for dynamic and heterogeneous environments
  • Platform engineering approaches enabling continuum-native development
  • Automated deployment and CI/CD strategies across distributed sites
  • Policy-driven and intent-based DevOps automation
  • SLO/SLA-driven multi-layer orchestration and adaptive DevOps workflows

🧪Development & Testing in the Cloud Continuum

  • New testing strategies for distributed, mobile, and ephemeral services
  • Constrained-device oriented development and testing
  • Fault-injection, chaos engineering, and resilience testing
  • Digital twins for development, test, and operational validation
  • Observability tools enabling deep visibility into continuum environments

🤖AI, ML Ops & LLM Ops Across the Continuum

  • MLOps solutions enabling distributed training, inference, and adaptation
  • Lightweight ML and MLOps for IoT/Edge deployments, including TinyML pipelines for on-device inference on resource-constrained hardware
  • ML/LLM-driven DevOps automation and intelligent orchestration
  • Anomaly detection, self-healing, and autonomous orchestration systems
  • Cloud Continuum-native ML pipelines and federated learning orchestration
  • SLM/LLM lifecycle management in heterogeneous environments

📡Next-Generation Networks & Continuum-Enabled 6G Scenarios

  • Cloud-continuum DevOps for 6G/NGN programmable infrastructures
  • DevOps in Low Power and LPWAN Networks
  • Integration of RAN, core cloud, and edge computing in DevOps workflows
  • Network slicing, micro-slicing, and continuum-aware network functions

🌆Use Cases, Vertical Domains & Emerging Scenarios

  • Smart City and Industry 5.0 applications
  • Autonomous systems, cyber-physical systems and distributed sensing ecosystems
  • Immersive media, AR/VR/XR, metaverse, and digital twins
